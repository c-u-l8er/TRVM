## The verification ledger, round 4 — every review claim confirmed, one of them twice over

**1. "The strategy is the progress certificate" was wrong — confirmed, and it's a retraction, not a relabel.** Round 3's L-PROG-1 read four naive schedulers failing the same six `church_exp` terms at a 20k budget as *slowness*, versus normal-order's 24/24 within 91 interactions. GPT called the reading into question; the diagnosis is worse than the question. All six failures are **false quiescence**: `findRedexes()` returns empty at steps **15/26/21/37/37/27** — nowhere near the budget — having consumed almost no interactions, and the reference driver, resumed from those very states, then burns a **2,000,000** budget in a sub-map knot firing ~0 interactions. The quiescent exp_2_2 state is small enough to stare at: `!&1001{a,b}=!&1002{c,d}=b;&1001{c,(e (f &1002{d,g}))};h`. Free scheduling on that relation wasn't slow; it was *unsound* — it reached states the enumeration couldn't see out of. L-PROG-1 is retired; the witness stays red by design as **L-FQ-AST-1** (`law:sched.free.ast-term@1`, FALSIFIED), and the grid carries the full retraction under `l_prog_history`. A red row must be diagnosed before it is narrated: v0.3 drew a true-sounding moral from an undiagnosed failure, inside the round whose theme was the record catching itself.

**2. The seam is the representation, not the planes — the stronger half of the finding.** The review prescribed separating INTERACT from COLLAPSE; I implemented the partition (`law:plane.rule-partition@1`) and then ran the sharper experiment first: free scheduling restricted to *pure INTERACT rules only* still knots **18/24**. Plane mixing was never the seam — the body-carrying AST term representation was. So the freedom claim moved to where it can be true: a **floating-dup heap** (`floating-dup-heap-v1`: dups extruded to a heap, the tree kept dup-free, `chase` through heap values). On that representation the theorem-shape is visible — `chase(dup.val)` can never yield a Dup, so the knot class is unrepresentable — and the batteries agree: **96/96** runs (4 schedulers × 24 vectors) complete, NF-match, **0** budget-outs, worst case **91 steps** where the AST battery needed 20k and still lost. The six exp terms complete at *exactly* the reference interaction counts — **21/42/36/91/71/55** — and L-DERIV-2 now shows something I did not expect: the float relation matches the spec's embedded counts **24/24** while the JS normal-order conformance driver itself matches them only **18/24**. The certified relation is more faithful to the spec than the driver that tests it (`law:deriv.count-invariance.float@1`). Freedom is a property of RELATION + REPRESENTATION — and it is now *stated* that way, nowhere else.

**3. The harness caught its builder twice in one day — both catches are laws now.** Building the float engine, the suite flagged two bugs the probe scripts had missed. First, **heap-invisible redexes**: enumeration initially saw only the root tree, missing App redexes inside heap-dup *values*; the NF batteries diverged immediately, and enumeration is now total over the live pool (locus type `v:<id>:<path>` exists so films can even *name* those redexes). Second, and better: **dead-dup affinity corruption**. A fuzz term — `(λa.!&500{b,c}=λd.!&501{e,f}=d;a;(b c) λg.(!&502{h,i}=λj.g;h &503{(X Z),(S X)}))` — normalized to `λa.b` under one schedule and `λa.a` under the reference. The cause: heap dups no longer reachable from the root (**dead**) could still fire on values *shared* with live dups, double-consuming affine variables. The fix is not a GC optimization, it is a theorem of the representation: **garbage must not compute** — enumeration ranges over LIVE ids only, and replay's digest chain refuses any film that fires a dead locus. Both bugs would have shipped silently without the falsifier suite; both are exactly the class of negative result this project exists to farm.

**4. The AST relation's false quiescence violated precisely this purity: its readback fired INTERACT and diverged — the two retractions are one phenomenon. L-COLLAPSE-PROG-1 confirms the class exists beyond the curated vectors: **84** fuzz quiescent states, **8** carrying live residuals, all readback-pure, all NF-matched. And the planes composition came out *measured* rather than assumed: phased INTERACT→COLLAPSE is an **interleaved fixpoint**, not a pipeline — collapse re-enables interact (copying a stuck application exposes fresh APP redexes) in 12 of 48 runs, max alternation depth **4** (`law:plane.separation.fixpoint@1`).
