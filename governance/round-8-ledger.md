## The verification ledger, round 8

**1. The falsifier came first, per house rules.** Composition's failure mode was designed before its success path: warrant B cites warrant A by reading A's *publication* (`warrant:A` = {value, warrant_id, footprint_id}); A goes phantom-stale by round-7's route — the world grows a member no exact read of A ever touched — and A's publication resource never moves, because **staleness is a relation to the world, not a write**. So naive B's only exact read is byte-identical and B reports `fresh` while transitively wrong. L-COMP-1 constructs exactly this (A `scope_dirty`, true size 3 vs published 2, naive B `fresh`) and keeps it red by construction: the composition phantom, one level up from round 7's.

**2. The repair: freshness itself becomes a reified scope.** `publishWarrant` registers `warrant-fresh:<name>` — a scope query whose result is the citee's footprint *re-evaluated against the current world* (exact versions + scope digests). A guarded composite's footprint pairs every publication read with that scope, so it dirties the moment the citee's evidence moves — no publication write required. L-COMP-2 proves propagation where it's hardest: a diamond (`D ← {A2, B2} ← C2`), one ground deletion, `D` classifies `support_changed` and **every** citer classifies `scope_dirty` along both paths — with the footprint deduplicating the join (each publication and each scope appears exactly once in C2's footprint, asserted). The scopes compose transitively for free: C2's `warrant-fresh:A2` re-evaluates A2's footprint, which contains `warrant-fresh:D`, which re-evaluates D's (`law:warrant.composition@1`).

**3. The jail is the abstraction boundary, doing double duty.** A composite's footprint contains publications and freshness scopes and **no citee internals** — asserted structurally in L-COMP-3 — so jailed replay proves the composite used only published values: an honest replay passes; a replayer that reaches for `node:a` refuses `undeclared-read` naming the resource; pruning the publication itself (honestly resealed) refuses `undeclared-read` at `warrant:A`. Round 5's law — authority must not outrun evidence — now polices module boundaries, not just read sets. En route, both replayers were hardened: a jail violation latches and subsequent reads return `undefined`, so an arbitrary measure function may throw before the refusal surfaces — the replayer now owns that (`try/catch`: a latched violation *is* the cause; a bare throw refuses `derivation-threw`, the ninth declared refusal). And the frame battery's random junk writes exposed that a world is untyped storage: the flagship measure now treats an ill-typed adjacency as carrying no edges rather than crashing the honest builder.

**4. σ T4, executable at last.** The frame rule stops being an analogy: L-FRAME-1 seals a warrant, performs a random write, and requires a *classifier* — footprint membership plus scope influence — to **predict the freshness verdict exactly**: outside both → `fresh`; scope influence only → `scope_dirty`; support hit → `support_changed`; non-support exact hit → `support_intact`. 120/120 agreement (23 writes inside the evidence, 97 outside). Writes outside a warrant's evidence cannot change its verdict — the separation-logic frame rule as a world property (`law:warrant.frame@1`), and the grid's new `sigma_profile` section records the T1 (derivation_id), T2 (vclock monotonicity), and T4 (frame) world-layer instantiations that the schemas have been naming since round 3.

**5. The record enforces the pairing rule engine-free.** Receipt v2 ships a ground warrant *and* a composite; grid_check v2.6 recomputes both warrant_ids, both footprint_ids, and the receipt_id from the receipt's own fields, and enforces the **pairing rule** structurally: every `warrant:<x>` exact read in the composite's footprint must have its `warrant-fresh:<x>` scope — a shipped receipt exhibiting the laundering pattern fails the checker with the words "staleness laundering" in the diagnostic. The negative battery grew to **31 forgeries, 31 distinct diagnostics**, including the pairing prune, a resealed composite value flip, and a stale-at-emit composite.

**6. Gate.** Both executables exit 0 (kernel untouched at v1.0.2 — frozen means frozen); grid 36 entries / 242 citations / 11 artifacts; κ green; battery 31/31; cert, refinement, and world receipts deterministic across double runs.

## Theory movement this round

**Publication ≠ freshness, and conflating them is the composition bug.** A publication is a *value* claim; freshness is a *relation to the current world*. The naive composite trusts the first to carry the second, and the phantom shows what that costs. Reifying freshness as a scope makes the relation itself an observable in the footprint — after which propagation, diamonds, and transitivity need no new machinery at all: they are just scope re-evaluation.

**One jail, three jobs.** The same jailed replay now enforces read soundness (round 7), module abstraction (this round), and — via `derivation-threw` — the honesty of the derivation function itself. Evidence discipline keeps collapsing into a single mechanism, which is what a law kernel is supposed to feel like.

**Next:** warrant *maintenance* (re-derive-on-dirty as a supervised operation over the DAG — the incremental-computation loop, with receipts); CP5–CP7's canonicalization-interleaved-with-world-transition obligation, for which the natural construction is a kernel-computation warrant (the kernel gains a module interface at 1.1.0 so the world can derive warrants whose witnesses are semantic films — unifying the two portable evidence objects); and ic32, which now has three protocol surfaces waiting: semantic films, warrants, and their composition.

## Round 8.1 — the audit's substrate catch, closed as the world's identity law

**7. GPT falsified the ground under everything above, and the witness reproduced exactly.** `AUDIT-WORLD-ALIAS`: the store held JS values *by reference*, so `world.read("r:x").value.push(2)` mutated the world with **no put, no version movement, no vclock movement** — and the two verification mechanisms disagreed: `freshness → fresh`, `replayWarrant → value-mismatch`. Reproduced byte-for-byte against v0.2.0 (frozen in `probe_worldalias_v02_repro.mjs`), plus the ingress twin (`{a:{b:[1,99]}}` leaked through a retained reference) and the deletion collapse (deleted resources read back at version 0 while the vclock said 2). The audit's framing is adopted whole: this is the world-layer analog of round 6's execution-vs-semantic identity problem — **evidence cannot outrun the state identity of the world it describes** — and the law beneath warrant freshness is not "versions increase" but *every observable mutation induces an identity transition*.

**8. Closed as a law with a canonical substrate, not a clone call.** `law:world.write-mediated@1`: same version ⇒ same canonical value. Mechanism, per the audit's stronger option: a **canonical resource-value domain** (null, boolean, finite number, string, arrays, plain objects with sorted keys — six refusal classes tested, each dying on `world-value-not-canonical` with no junk resource created); the store owns canonical **bytes** under `TRVM-VALUE-v1` content hashes; every read parses a fresh copy — both alias directions die at the boundary. Deletions become **tombstones** carrying the deletion version, so "deleted at 52" and "never existed" no longer collapse, and the full lifecycle (absent → put → update → delete → recreate) is a battery. The corollary the witness falsified is its own law — `law:warrant.fresh-replay-coherence@1`: **FRESH ⇒ REPLAY-COHERENT** — property-tested over 200 random histories laced with adversarial alias attempts: every fresh verdict (151/151) replays ok, with the per-history version⇒hash invariant cross-checked against the store's own log. (First draft of that battery keyed the invariant *across* 200 independent worlds and falsified itself on colliding coordinates — the invariant quantifies over one history; fixed and noted.)

**9. Two confessions, recorded rather than repaired silently.** The audit caught `... === 49 - 0 || true` in L-WORLD-1 — an assertion *incapable of failing*, in the very battery that introduced the monotonicity law. It is gone; the battery now checks latest-read on all six live resources, the tombstone lifecycle, and version⇒hash; and the evidence line confesses the vacuity. And an honesty note on sequencing: round 8's composition layer was built before this audit arrived — it is not retracted, because its laws never depended on the aliasing hole, but every composition battery **re-ran green on the write-mediated substrate**, which is the re-validation the audit's stop-the-line call demanded. All thirteen world batteries pass on v0.3.0; the kernel remains frozen at v1.0.2.

**The rhyme, stated once.** Round 5: authority must not outrun evidence. Round 6: identity must know what it identifies. Round 8.1: evidence cannot outrun the identity of the world it describes. Each layer's first falsifier has been the same law wearing that layer's clothes — which is either a deep fact about evidence-carrying computation or a very persistent reviewer. The record can afford to say: both.

## Round 8.2 — support was committed but never proven; closed at all three verifiers

**10. GPT falsified support authority, and every step of the witness reproduced.** `AUDIT-SUPPORT-LAUNDER` against v0.3.0 (frozen in `probe_support_v03_repro.mjs`): a warrant with honest support `["r:x"]`, forged to `[]` with an *honest reseal*, **replayed ok** — replay checked only `support ⊆ footprint`, never `out.support == w.support`. Then `r:x` moved: the honest warrant classified `support_changed`, the forged one `support_intact` — *same derivation, same world change, same evidence, different invalidation theorem*. Then the worst part: `refreshWarrant` succeeded and **preserved the forged support** (`{...w}` kept the old field), so the false classification laundered through the exact gate the maintenance loop would trust. And the receipt version — support pruned, both ids honestly recomputed — **passed grid_check**, whose subset check proves necessity, not truth. Three verifiers, zero establishing support truth: the round-5 assurance-composition species, one layer up. Also confirmed en route: `replayComposite` had *neither* support check.

**11. Closed as `law:warrant.support-soundness@1`, with the division of verification extended.** Support is **canonical at seal** (sorted, deduplicated — reordering is a no-op by construction, tested). Both replayers now enforce **both properties**: `support ⊆ exact reads` (structural, pre-derivation) *and* `support == canonical(out.support)` (semantic, post-derivation — `support-mismatch`, the tenth refusal). `refreshWarrant` **restores derivation-produced support** — the laundering chain breaks at its own gate. For the shipped receipt, the world artifact gained its engine mode: the receipt commits a `world_spec` (`TRVM-WORLDRECEIPT-v3`), and `trvm_world.mjs --check-receipt` rebuilds the world from it and *replays both warrants* — the audit's exact receipt attack now dies there with `support-mismatch`, while grid_check v2.8 holds the engine-free half honestly (commitments, subset, and a new **canonical-form** check that catches unsorted forgeries). One acknowledged limit, stated rather than hidden: `freshness()` alone cannot see a support forgery without the engine — the gates that decide anything (replay, refresh, receipt check) all re-derive.

**12. L-SUPPORT-1 locks the chain verbatim, and one battery got sharper.** The locked regression is GPT's exact sequence: forge `[]` → replay **refuses** `support-mismatch` → `r:x` moves → the classification split is asserted *as the stated limit* → refresh **restores** `["r:x"]` → the refreshed warrant replays ok and classifies `support_changed` on the next movement. Six forgeries follow, each on its declared refusal: prune, nonsemantic-read inflation (a read-but-not-semantic resource added), duplicate entry (canonical form), outside-footprint (structural), composite prune, composite inflation. The stricter replay also caught L-COMP-3's old expectation: the pruned-publication forgery now dies *earlier* (`support-not-subset`, structural), so the battery was sharpened to also run the **fully consistent** forgery — publication pruned from footprint *and* support — which must still die at the jail with `undeclared-read`. The same forgery now dies twice over, at two different layers, and the battery says which.

**13. Gate.** Fourteen world batteries green (L-SUPPORT-1 plus all thirteen re-runs), `--check-receipt` green on the honest receipt and refusing the attack, grid v1.4.0 — 39 entries / 271 citations, κ green, negative battery **35/35** (including the first *engine-side* case, run through the new `run_case_engine` runner), receipts deterministic.
