## Theory movement this round

**Adequacy joins as Equivariance's dual, with a genealogy of real failures.** Equivariance: don't distinguish irrelevant differences (the κ tie bug, label leakage). Adequacy: don't erase relevant differences — P(x)=P(y) ⇒ O(x)=O(y) — which is exactly what film digest v1 violated and what the old scalar κ violated, and which is the sharpened form of the ecosystem's existing Law 6. The kernel now tests the duals as separate rows (L-ADEQ-1, L-EQV-1) sharing one computation. The meta-law family — Equivariance, Adequacy, Closure, Coherence, Refinement, Well-foundedness, Progress — now has the property GPT rightly demanded before anyone claims a periodic law: nearly every member entered through a counterexample in a real artifact, not through category brainstorming.

**Film v3 shipped.** Frames bind pre *and* post canonical state ids and hash-chain (`frame_id = H(prev | pre | rule | path | post)`); films carry a terminal record with termination status (NORMAL_FORM / BUDGET_EXHAUSTED), step count, and last frame; replay verifies both state ids and recomputes the chain end to end, refusing with typed reasons (`revision-mismatch`, `post-state-mismatch`, `chain-mismatch`). The termination statuses matter now precisely because the progress work made NORMAL_FORM and BUDGET_EXHAUSTED semantically different outcomes of the same confluent rules.
