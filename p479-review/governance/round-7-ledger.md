## The verification ledger, round 7

**1. The kernel is frozen; the layer above it now exists.** The closure audit's recommendation — freeze the calculus kernel and move upward — is followed literally: v1.0.2 differs from v1.0.1 by a version constant, a header paragraph, and citation strings (the identity law revved under it, see item 6); zero semantic changes. The new construction is a **separate executable**, `trvm_world.mjs` v0.1.0, because the kernel has no world *by design* and the WORLD plane was always declared to live beyond it.

**2. What a warrant turned out to be, with the literature's help.** Reading before building: the Build-Systems-à-la-Carte trace taxonomy (verifying traces = deps as key/hash pairs, freshness by re-fetch-and-compare; constructive traces add the value; **early cutoff** when a changed dep re-derives an unchanged value), the Adapton family (demanded computation graphs, nominal stable identifiers, and the documented weakness of dirty-bit phase separation that version-exact footprints avoid), and the databases' answer to phantoms (predicate locks / SSI) plus the build systems' equivalent (tracked globs as first-class keys). The grid's Warrant v3 shape is exactly the common generalization: **a portable verifying trace whose footprint carries both exact reads `[resource@version]` and reified predicate scopes `[query@digest]`, with a jailed replayer as its soundness proof.** All four anchors are recorded in the grid's `world.research_anchors`.

**3. The WorldRecord and the two views.** A versioned resource store on a global monotone vclock with an append-only dense log (`law:world.version-monotone@1`, L-WORLD-1), plus registered scope queries whose evaluations are *reified* — a digest of the canonical result set under domain `TRVM-SCOPE-v1`. A predicate observation is itself a versioned observable, not an ambient fact. Derivations run under a **tracked view** (the honest builder: every read and every scope evaluation lands in the footprint) or a **jailed view** (the replayer: anything outside the declared footprint refuses). The flagship world is κ-flavored: nodes, edges, and forward adjacency indices as resources; component-size measures whose footprints are exactly their traversals.

**4. Freshness that ignores noise, and a jail that catches pruning.** L-WAR-1: a component-size warrant is fresh at seal and **still fresh after 50 unrelated writes** — freshness reads the footprint, not the world's write counter (`law:warrant.freshness@1`). L-WAR-2 is the authority-must-not-outrun-evidence law applied to reads (`law:warrant.footprint-soundness@1`): honest jailed replay passes; a **pruned footprint, honestly resealed**, dies at the jail with `undeclared-read`; an inflated value dies at `value-mismatch`; an unsealed mutation both diverges the commitment and fails re-derivation; a support set exceeding the footprint dies at `support-not-subset`. Eight typed refusals, all presence-checked by grid_check against the grid's declaration.

**5. The phantom is no longer a sentence in a spec.** The grid has said since round 3 that predicate scopes "guard the phantom-read case κ exposed, where a node absent at read time joins the SCC without touching any old edge." L-WAR-3 **constructs it**: a writer adds `node:c`, the edge resource, and the *reverse* index while leaving the forward adjacency the derivation actually read untouched — every exact read byte-identical, true component size 3, warranted value 2, and the unguarded warrant reports `fresh`. Kept red by construction, like every falsifier in this record. The guarded warrant — one reified `incident:` scope in its footprint — classifies `scope_dirty` on the same world (`law:warrant.phantom-scope@1`). Exact reads alone cannot witness membership of a set that grew; that sentence is now a passing battery, not a caution.

**6. Staleness is a trichotomy with witnesses, and one class is cheap.** L-WAR-4 produces each verdict in its own world with its complete witness (the round-6.1 terminal-witness law, applied to verdicts): `support_changed` names the moved resource; `scope_dirty` names the scope and both digests; `support_intact` — a read-but-not-support resource reversioned with identical content — triggers **early cutoff**: jailed re-derivation reproduces the value, the footprint reversions, the warrant reseals, and the refreshed warrant is fresh (`law:warrant.invalidation-trichotomy@1`). The verifying-trace optimization, as a warrant *operation* rather than a build-system internal.

**7. One evidence language — the round-5 requirement discharged.** L-WAR-5 wraps the shipped 144-receipt SchedulerCertificate as a Warrant v3: its `corpus{id,sha256}` becomes one exact read of a corpus resource, its evidence the value, its run-manifest hash the witness. Fresh at seal, fresh under unrelated writes, `support_changed` exactly when the corpus resource moves (`law:footprint.shared@1`). Certificate freshness *is* warrant freshness; the footprint is the shared shape round 5 asked for.

**8. Identity became a map, and the checker made the rev honest.** With a second executable, the round-6 lockstep (grid.version == kernel constant) is the wrong shape: `kernel.identity@2` supersedes `@1` — every artifact declares a version constant, the grid carries `artifact_versions`, grid_check v2.5 parses each constant from source and locks the map, and `grid.version` becomes the record's own lineage. The supersession immediately proved the discipline works: six stale `@1` citations lit up across the grid, the kernel, and the round-6 ledger — fixed by the hygiene rev, two superseded-safe annotations, and this ledger's existence (which freezes round-6's as history). **The stale-citation tripwire caught the record's own author within minutes of the rev.** That is what it is for.

**9. First-run pass, skeptically re-derived.** All six world batteries passed on first execution — which, per this record's own history, is grounds for suspicion, not celebration. Re-review confirmed each assertion bites: the latest-read check pins the actual last write (`r1@43`); the churn immunity spans 50 writes including new edges; the phantom's wrong-answer is recomputed from ground truth (`trueSize !== naked.value` asserted, not assumed); the early-cutoff case asserts value preservation *and* id reseal *and* post-refresh freshness. The receipt (`world_warrant_receipt.json`) is deterministic across runs, and grid_check recomputes its warrant_id, footprint_id, and receipt_id engine-free from the receipt's own fields — the round-5 division of verification, extended to the new layer on day one.

## Theory movement this round

**A warrant is a verifying trace that learned about phantoms and got a jail.** The exact-read half is the build-systems idea; the predicate-scope half is the databases' idea; the jailed replay is this record's own rounds-5/6 law — authority must not outrun evidence — applied to *reads*. The three compose into the grid's four-year-old shape without remainder, which is the kind of convergence worth writing down.

**Support ≠ footprint is where the economics live.** Everything read is footprint; only what the value semantically depends on is support. The gap between them is exactly the early-cutoff opportunity (`support_intact`), and the trichotomy prices staleness: cheap refresh, forced re-derivation, or phantom alarm.

**Next:** the σ profile instantiated against the world layer (T1 derivation / T2 monotonicity / T4 frame-rule locality already have their instances waiting in L-WORLD/L-WAR); CP5–CP7's carrier-scoped coherence obligation — "interleaving canonicalization with world transition" — now has a world to interleave *with*; warrant composition (a derivation reading another warrant's value: footprint union, support propagation); and ic32 against the semantic-film protocol, which gained a sibling this round — the warrant is to WORLD what the semantic film is to the calculus: the portable evidence object.
