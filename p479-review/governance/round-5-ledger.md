## Theory movement this round

**Authority may not outrun evidence — now a design law, instantiated twice.** The certificate's envelope claimed more than its checker re-derived; the film's replay accepted more than its relation permits. Both fixes have the same shape: *the verifier recomputes the world, then the commitment* — receipts re-executed, frames re-enabled — so a laundered commitment buys an adversary nothing. The reduction theorem itself never wobbled: 96/96, max 91 steps, counts spec-equal 24/24, all reproduced independently by the reviewer. The round's outcome is the methodology's advertised failure mode working as designed: **the math survived; what failed was the machinery claiming permission from the math.**

**Exhibit ≠ evidence basis.** Six films were never the support set for a 96-run claim; they are witnesses. The support set is the receipt manifest, and the claim chain now reads `claim → law_refs → claims vocabulary → receipts → films/digests → corpus → relation identity`. GPT's observation that this converges with the warrant machinery is adopted as a design constraint: **SchedulerCertificate and Warrant v3 should share a support/footprint concept** rather than growing two evidence languages — that is now a stated requirement for the warrant round, not an aesthetic preference.

**Self-correction is symmetric.** The same falsifier discipline that retracted L-PROG-1 in round 4 revised my own round-4 diagnosis in round 5 — with the original divergence replicated under its exact seeds to make the revision *measured* rather than narrated. The grid records the correction next to the original claim; neither is overwritten.
